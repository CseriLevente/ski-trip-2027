Reading the numbers

Drive times are best-case

They come from OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap roads: a vehicle with no traffic, snow or parking. On a snowy February morning, add 20–40% and time to park. Both stays are on flat valley roads with parking at the house, and the main lifts all have free open-air car parks.

Kitzsteinhorn starts in Kaprun

The Maiskogelbahn in Kaprun village connects to the glacier by lift (the 3K K-onnection), so that's where the times point. Driving to the glacier base station (Gletscherjet) takes 15 min from the Tauernhütte and 13 from Wood 2.

Skicircus: two ways in

From both stays, the quickest way in is the Schönleitenbahn at Vorderglemm (27 and 25 min); Saalbach centre is about 4 minutes further. The zellamseeXpress at Viehhofen goes up Schmittenhöhe from the Glemm valley side; there's no ski link from it into the Skicircus.
